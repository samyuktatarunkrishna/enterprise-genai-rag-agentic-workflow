=========================================
ENTERPRISE SUITE PRODUCT KNOWLEDGE BASE
=========================================

1. Core Product Features
The Enterprise Suite is a cloud-native platform featuring real-time collaborative workspaces, AI-driven data intelligence, automated workflow pipelines, and enterprise-grade security. Key features include the Multi-Tenant Dashboard, custom integration APIs, secure data connectors, and the advanced Analytics Engine. The engine processes terabytes of structured and unstructured data to provide actionable business intelligence charts.

2. Target Customers and Use Cases
Our product is tailored for mid-market and global enterprise organizations. Common customer use cases include:
- Financial Reporting: Automating regulatory reporting and auditing schedules.
- Marketing Orchestration: Managing cross-channel campaigns and lead attribution.
- IT System Monitoring: Tracking network bandwidth, server health, and software deployment statuses.
- Customer Support: Managing support tickets and routing them to specialized agents.

3. Pricing Tiers and Subscriptions
We offer three standard pricing tiers designed to scale with organization size:
- Professional Tier: $50 per user/month, includes up to 50 users, standard dashboard, basic integrations, and 8x5 email support.
- Business Tier: $120 per user/month, includes up to 250 users, advanced analytics, custom connectors, and 24x7 chat support.
- Enterprise Tier: Custom pricing, includes unlimited users, dedicated deployment, priority API access, compliance auditing, and 24x7 phone support.

4. Customer Support Options
Support levels vary by subscription tier, but all customers can access our online Documentation Hub and Community Forums. For paid tiers, support tickets can be submitted via the Support Portal. Professional and Business tier customers have access to email and chat support during standard business hours. Enterprise tier customers receive a dedicated Technical Account Manager (TAM) and a 1-hour SLA response time for critical issues.
