=========================================
ENTERPRISE GLOBAL HR POLICIES & BENEFITS
=========================================

1. Employee Onboarding Process
Every new employee must complete the standard onboarding process within their first week. This includes completing the compliance training modules, submitting payroll and banking information, and setting up their profile on the HR Portal. Managers are required to schedule a 30-60-90 day check-in with new hires to ensure alignment on team goals, clear performance metrics, and smooth integration into the team.

2. Annual Leave and Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time employees are eligible for 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year, accrued monthly. Up to 5 unused PTO days can be rolled over to the next calendar year, but must be used by March 31st. Any additional unused PTO is forfeited unless local laws state otherwise. To request leave, employees must submit a request through the HR Portal at least two weeks in advance. For sick leave exceeding three consecutive days, a doctor's certificate is required.

3. Remote Work and Flexible Schedules
Our organization supports a hybrid remote work model. Employees can request remote work by submitting a formal proposal to their direct manager and local HR lead. Approval is based on role requirements, performance history, and operational needs. Remote employees are expected to maintain core working hours from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time to support team collaboration. A home office stipend is available for ergonomic setups and internet upgrades.

4. Employee Benefits and Wellness Programs
We provide comprehensive health insurance covering medical, dental, and vision care. Additionally, the wellness program offers gym membership subsidies, mental health counseling sessions through the Employee Assistance Program (EAP), and annual health screenings. The EAP is confidential and available 24/7. Employees are also eligible for educational assistance, matching 401(k) retirement contributions up to 4%, and paid parental leave.
